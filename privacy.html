Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how DND TOOLBOX uses data inside your Figma project, and what the developer can and cannot access.

Developer: PhosphixLast updated: 24 October 2026Plugin version: 0.5.1

In short: Your campaign stays in your Figma project. DND TOOLBOX does not upload it to Phosphix or operate a separate game-data server. If you email the developer for support, your message is handled separately.

1. Overview

DND TOOLBOX is an independent Figma plugin for tabletop role-playing games. It helps users manage character sheets, inventories, notes, tokens, dice rolls, and combat initiative in their own Figma documents.

Phosphix does not collect or receive users' campaign data through the plugin. DND TOOLBOX has no developer-operated server, user database, analytics, advertising, or tracking system.

2. Data used to make the plugin work

Depending on the features used, DND TOOLBOX processes information within Figma, including:

  • Figma user identifiers and display names, when available, to distinguish the game master and players and associate characters with their users;
  • Character sheets, imported character JSON, stats, inventory, notes, dice results, combat state, and initiative;
  • Character portraits, token images, and references to objects on the Figma canvas;
  • User preferences, recent dice history, and some private game-master information.

This information is used to provide the features requested by users, not to build profiles or deliver advertising.

3. Where data is stored and who can see it

Shared campaign data is stored in the user's Figma document using Figma's plugin-data features. Token shapes and images are stored on the Figma canvas. Some settings, dice history, and private game-master rolls use Figma plugin client storage.

Figma provides document storage, sharing, and synchronization. Other people with access to the same Figma file may be able to access its contents according to Figma's permissions. The file owner controls whom to invite. Phosphix cannot access a user's campaign merely because the plugin is installed.

Hidden enemies are concealed in the plugin's player interface only. This is not encryption or a guarantee that the data cannot be inspected by a person with access to the shared Figma file.

4. No transmission to the developer

DND TOOLBOX v0.5.1 does not send campaign data to Phosphix or to third-party analytics services. Its manifest disallows external network domains. The Figma platform itself is an online service and handles its own storage and network activity under Figma's privacy terms.

Support is separate: If you choose to email phosphixxx@gmail.com, Phosphix will receive your email address and the information you include in your message through the email provider. Please do not send private campaign data unless it is needed to resolve your request.

5. Your controls and data retention

You can edit or remove game information using the plugin's available controls and Figma's file controls. Some technical events, copies, backups, and version history may remain within Figma according to its features and policies. Phosphix does not operate a separate campaign database and therefore cannot directly retrieve or delete records from your Figma file.

Support emails are kept only as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact Phosphix to request information about, or deletion of, support correspondence handled by the developer.

6. Third-party services

DND TOOLBOX depends on Figma for document access, storage, and collaboration. If this policy is hosted through GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary website-access information under its own privacy terms. This policy page itself uses no advertising, analytics, cookies set by the developer, or external scripts.

7. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated if the plugin's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

For privacy questions or support, contact phosphixxx@gmail.com.
